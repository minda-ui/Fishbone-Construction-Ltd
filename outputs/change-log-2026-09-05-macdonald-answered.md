# 2026-09-05 - Macdonald Joinery: the owner answered why

The top open question in this knowledge base, listed as such in `CLAUDE.md` §7, on
`Wiki/index.md` and on the article itself, was why Macdonald Joinery's payments fell 86% in May
2026. Five months of reconciled bank data could not distinguish between five candidate
explanations. The owner answered it in one sentence:

> "forget Macdonald, they don't have projects"

## Raw registered

- `Raw/2026-09-05_owner-note_macdonald-joinery-has-no-projects.md` - the statement written up
  per `CLAUDE.md` §1, with statement and commentary separated, so the wiki can cite it rather
  than citing a conversation.

## What it settles

Four of the five candidates are eliminated. It was **not** a contract completing, not a contract
lost to a competitor, not work in dispute, and not payment being withheld. There is no debtor to
chase, no dispute to resolve and no damaged relationship to repair.

**It is the explanation with the fewest routes back.** Every other candidate had an action
attached to it. This one does not: the revenue returns when the customer's own demand returns,
and Fishbone neither controls that nor, on the evidence here, has visibility of it.

## What it does not settle, recorded so nobody later assumes it did

- **Whether it is temporary.** The statement describes a state now, not a forecast. Until
  somebody asks Macdonald, no projection should assume recovery from this customer.
- **What the residual ~£8,000 a month is.** Steady for three months, which is not what a stopped
  relationship looks like. Could be a small continuing job, a payment plan against an older
  balance, or a retention release. Unexamined.
- **Whether anything is still owed.** No receivable position has been checked. The QuickBooks
  A/R detail would answer it and does not depend on the stale bank feed.

## The question that took its place

**What replaces the revenue?**

This was implicit before and is now unavoidable. 80% of third-party receipts came from one
customer who has no work, and **no other customer, contract, quote or pipeline is recorded
anywhere in this knowledge base** - not in `Raw/`, not in the wiki, not in the Smartsheet
project register as far as it has been read. The concentration risk did not merely exist; it
has already materialised.

The second question changed character rather than moving up. Fishbone Properties Ltd and the
directors have put in £319,460 over six months, about £53,000 a month. If any of that was
advanced in expectation that Macdonald would come back, the basis for it has gone. Whether that
expectation was ever held, or discussed, is not recorded anywhere.

## A note on how this was answered

Five months of bank statements, categorised and reconciled to the penny, established *what*
happened to within a single statement month and could not establish *why*. One sentence from
the owner did.

That is not an argument against the data work - the analysis is what made the question precise
enough to be worth asking, and it is what will tell anyone whether the answer holds. But it is
worth recording that the cheapest of the four routes listed on the article, "ask the director",
was the one that worked, and that it was listed fourth.
