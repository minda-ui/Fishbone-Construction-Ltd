# Change log - 2026-09-12 - Second vehicle confirmed as a company van

## What was asked

Following the 2026-09-11 flag on `FCT00020` and `Wiki/Assets/vehicles.md` about a one-letter
difference between the MOT certificate's registration (`NJ17CYK`) and the DVLA tax reminder's
registration (`NJ17 CWK`), the owner said, referring to `FCT00020`:

> "This is one of our company vans. No issues with registration plate number"

## What was done

**Wrote up the owner's statement** as `Raw/2026-09-12_owner-note_vehicle-fct00020-confirmed-
company-van.md`, statement and commentary separated per `CLAUDE.md` §1.

**Narrowed, rather than fully closed, the open question on `Wiki/Assets/vehicles.md`** - per
`CLAUDE.md` §6d rule 2, the original question is struck through and answered in place, not
deleted. The owner's statement settles that this is one genuine company vehicle (not two, and
not a mistake), but it does not itself state which of the two transcribed plates is spelled
correctly - that narrower question is kept open. The article now counts this as the **second of
three confirmed vehicles**.

**Updated `FCT00020`** (Smartsheet Tasks) with the owner's confirmation; retitled from
"registration discrepancy unresolved" to "vehicle confirmed, exact plate spelling still open".
**Updated `FCD0000025`**'s Document Register description similarly.

## What was not done

- **Did not pick a canonical spelling** for the registration. The owner's statement doesn't
  restate the plate itself, so `NJ17CYK` and `NJ17 CWK` both stay on record until a document in
  the owner's own words (or the V5C/insurance schedule) settles it.
- **No DVLA contact, taxing or SORN action.** The tax deadline (30 September 2026) and the
  governance reason (`CLAUDE.md` §6a - owner decision) are unchanged; only the registration
  question moved.
- **No change to the HSE invoice (`FC0000010`/`FCT00008`)** - unrelated to this update.
- **`CLAUDE.md` was not replaced.** Not a structural change (§6c).
