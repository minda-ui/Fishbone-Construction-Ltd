# Owner note - 2026-09-12 - Two vans, two registrations, both correct

## Statement

The owner (Minda), referring to the registration discrepancy flagged on `FCT00020` and
`Wiki/Assets/vehicles.md` between the MOT certificate (`NJ17CYK`) and the DVLA tax reminder
(`NJ17 CWK`):

> "We have two vans which reg plate have letter difference. NJ17CWK and NJ17CYK"

## What this answers

**This is two vehicles, not one.** The earlier working assumption - narrowed as far as "one
genuine company van, exact spelling of its plate still unconfirmed" by the owner's statement
earlier the same day (`Raw/2026-09-12_owner-note_vehicle-fct00020-confirmed-company-van.md`) -
is superseded. Both registrations are correct, each naming a distinct, genuine company van:

- **`NJ17CYK`** - the vehicle in the MOT certificate/refusal notice, `FCD0000025`.
- **`NJ17CWK`** - the vehicle in the DVLA V11 tax reminder, `FC0000011`/`FCT00020`.

This also fully accounts for the three vehicles the owner named on 2026-09-09: Ford Transit
`HT22BWP`, Ford Transit `NJ17CYK`, Ford Transit `NJ17CWK`.

## What this does not say

- **Does not confirm insurer, keeper, or finance arrangement** for either `NJ17CYK` or
  `NJ17CWK` individually - no hire-purchase facility for either has been found in the group
  Loans database (unlike `HT22BWP`, which is on record with MotoNovo Finance).
- **Does not confirm `NJ17CYK`'s tax status.** Only `NJ17CWK` has a DVLA tax reminder on file
  (expiring 30 September 2026); nothing here says whether `NJ17CYK`'s tax is current.
- **Does not authorise any DVLA contact, taxing, or SORN action.** The tax deadline for
  `NJ17CWK` (30 September 2026) is unchanged, and per `CLAUDE.md` §6a acting on it remains an
  owner decision.
- **Written as `NJ17CWK` (no space) here**, matching the MOT certificate's un-spaced format;
  the DVLA reminder itself printed it with a space (`NJ17 CWK`). Not treated as a substantive
  difference - vehicle registrations are not space-sensitive - but noted for anyone comparing
  the two documents directly.
