# Owner note, 2026-09-05 - Macdonald Joinery has no projects

Verbal statement by the owner (Mindaugas Gaudiesius, Managing Director), written up by the
assistant on 2026-09-05 so that the Wiki can cite it. Statement and commentary are separated
below, per `CLAUDE.md` §1.

## Statement

Asked why Macdonald Joinery's payments fell 86% in May 2026, the owner said:

> "forget Macdonald, they don't have projects"

## What this answers

The knowledge base had listed five candidate explanations for the fall and could not choose
between them from the bank data: a contract completed, a contract lost or cancelled, work in
dispute, payment withheld, or the customer in difficulty.

The answer is none of the first four. **Macdonald Joinery has no work to place.** The fall is
not a dispute, not a debtor problem, and not a single contract ending. There is nothing to
chase, recover or resolve.

## What this does not say

Recorded explicitly so that no later reading treats these as settled:

- **Whether it is temporary or permanent.** "They don't have projects" describes a state now.
  It does not say whether Macdonald expects work to return, or when.
- **Whether anything is still owed.** The steady ~£8,000 a month since May was not explained.
  It may be a small continuing job, a payment plan against an old balance, or something else.
- **Whether the relationship is intact.** Nothing in the statement suggests it is damaged; the
  owner's framing is that the customer has no demand, not that Fishbone lost the work.
- **What replaces the revenue.** Not addressed.

## Commentary (assistant, not the owner)

This is the answer with the fewest routes back. A dispute can be resolved, a withheld payment
can be chased, a completed contract can be followed by the next one. A customer with no
projects offers none of those: the revenue is gone until that customer's own demand returns, and
Fishbone does not control that.

Two consequences follow, and neither is addressed anywhere in this knowledge base:

1. **80% of third-party receipts came from one customer**, who now has no work. That is a
   concentration risk that has already materialised, and no article records any other customer
   being pursued or any pipeline.
2. **The related-party funding has no end date attached to it.** Fishbone Properties Ltd and the
   directors have put in £319,460 over six months. If that was being advanced in expectation
   that Macdonald would come back, the basis for it has changed.

Neither point is the owner's statement. Both are questions raised by it.
