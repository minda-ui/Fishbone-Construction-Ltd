# Kosmosoft SmartCABINET software purchase

A software purchase from an Italian vendor, found in `Raw/` on 2026-09-10 and confirmed paid by
the owner the same day. Registered locally as `FCD0000024` in the Construction Document
Register.

## What was bought

Offer/Contract Nr **22910**, dated 10/09/2026, from **Kosmosoft Engineering S.r.l.** (Via
Catania 1, 46031 Bagnolo San Vito (MN), Italy; VAT No. `02037680200`), addressed to **Fishbone
Drylining Ltd** - this company's name before the 31 October 2024 change to Fishbone Construction
Ltd. A vendor still using the old name is not evidence of anything beyond the vendor's own
records being out of date.

Product: **SmartCABINET**, described as "the ultimate CRM/CAD/CAM/ERP software for cabinet
[making]". One module is clearly legible in the OCR - **"Cut optimization"** - others are
present in the document but not cleanly readable.

Terms: payment 100% at confirmation; offer valid 30 days, then automatically cancelled if not
accepted; payment by bank transfer to a named Credem account (IBAN `IT18 H03032 11500
010000004086`). Travel/food/hotel expenses for installation are explicitly **not** included and
would be invoiced separately, 30 days after installation.

## Confirmed paid

The owner stated on 2026-09-10: *"22910 Fishbone is already paid"* - written up in full at
`Raw/2026-09-10_owner-note_kosmosoft-22910-paid.md`. This settled that the purchase went ahead;
it did not state an amount.

**Amount confirmed 2026-09-11: €1,000.** The owner stated *"It's 1000 euros"* - written up in
full at `Raw/2026-09-11_owner-note_kosmosoft-22910-price-confirmed.md`. This resolves the OCR
ambiguity below in favour of the single-figure reading (a duplicated "1.000,00" across the
scanned form's columns, not three separate €1,000 line items).
