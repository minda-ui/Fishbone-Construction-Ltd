# Assets category created for vehicles

`CLAUDE.md` §1 named eight wiki categories (Contracts, Customers, Decisions, Finance, People,
Processes, Projects, Suppliers). None fit company-owned physical assets - a vehicle isn't a
customer, a supplier, or a finance article about a facility (the facility itself already has a
home in the group Loans database). A ninth category, **Assets**, was added rather than forcing
vehicles into an existing one.

## Why not Finance

The temptation was to file this under `Finance/`, next to `debt-service.md` and
`funding-circle-flexipay.md`. Rejected: those articles are about the company's lending
position, sourced from the bank statements and authoritative in the group Loans database. A
vehicle article is about the asset - insurance, MOT, mileage, condition - which has nothing to
do with how it was financed. Keeping them apart also keeps the Loans database's authority over
lending facts intact: `Finance/` here would otherwise start duplicating what MotoNovo's HP
terms are, which is exactly the kind of copying `CLAUDE.md` §6f's "linked, not copied" rule
exists to prevent.

## Why now

The owner asked for a dedicated workspace for the company's three vehicles, with documents to
be uploaded to Drive and a Smartsheet register to follow once they're gathered. That's Raw
material (documents) and Wiki material (what's known) both needing a home that didn't exist.

## What this doesn't decide

**Whether Assets should also hold equipment.** The Haydock Finance HP (three woodworking
machines) is exactly the same shape of problem - a company-owned physical asset, financed, with
no natural category - and currently has no home outside the Loans database's finance-facing
write-up. Folding it into `Assets/` was considered and deliberately not done here: the owner's
request was specifically about vehicles, and building out a general equipment taxonomy nobody
asked for yet would be scope creep. Recorded as an open question on `Assets/vehicles.md` so the
next session doesn't have to rediscover the gap.
