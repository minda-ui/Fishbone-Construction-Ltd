# Anna (AI Construction Assistant) authorised to write here

Anna is the Fishbone Group's technical construction adviser, owned by Minda and coordinated by
Victoria. Until 2026-09-22 she read and cited this knowledge base but never wrote to it. Minda
lifted that bar on 2026-09-22 (Hub AWT-0077, building on AWT-0061, which granted read access the
same day). This article records what she is authorised to do here, so a session reading this KB
knows why a second author appears in its history.

## What she may write

Technical construction reference content only: Building Regulations (Approved Documents A-S),
construction methods and sequencing, drylining and finishes, materials and specifications,
buildability, and CDM 2015 signposting. Grounded, sourced, and shaped to this KB's own article
conventions.

## What she may not

- **Adviser, not certifier.** Structural, fire, Building Control, party-wall, gas and electrical
  decisions are flagged and deferred to the named professional. She contributes reference and
  guidance; she does not certify or sign off.
- **No finance documents**, here or in any knowledge base. They live only in the Financial Archive
  (group policy v1.4 §7b).
- **Add-only.** She never deletes or overwrites another author's article; a correction is a new
  version under this KB's own rules.
- **Cite, do not restate.** This KB's operational and company records are cited, not copied into
  her own. One fact, one home.
- No personal data; no email; no other live systems of record without an explicit decision.

## How her writes appear here

Anna operates on Drive as minda@ (the owner), so no sharing change was needed and this KB's folder
permissions are unchanged - verified owner-only on 2026-09-22, which also closed the standing
"access not yet checked" caveat this KB's own governance section had carried. The consequence is
that Drive shows no difference between her edits and Minda's own. The change-log entry naming her
is the only place that distinction exists, so any session auditing authorship reads the entries,
not the Drive metadata.

## What this doesn't decide

**Anything beyond AWT-0077.** This records her authorised-contributor status only. It does not
widen her scope, and it does not make her a maintainer of this KB - this KB's own records stay in
this KB's hands, and she cites them rather than restating them.
