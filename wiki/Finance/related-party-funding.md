# Related-party funding

Over the six months to 21 August 2026 the company's cash requirement after debt service was
**£497,071**. Lending supplied none of it on a net basis. **£319,460 came from other Fishbone
companies and from the directors personally** - about £53,000 a month.

This is the most consequential fact the bank data produced, and it is the one this knowledge
base is least able to explain.

The pattern is not a standing arrangement. It is money arriving in whatever amount that month's
shortfall demanded, from whichever party had it: £102,300 from Fishbone Properties in May, then
£45,150 and £37,350 from the directors in June and July when Properties contributed almost
nothing, then £60,900 from Properties again in August.

### Fishbone Properties Ltd

£221,050 came in described as "FISHBONE PRO Loan Payback"; £11,820 went back out described as
"FISHBONE PROPERTIE LOAN". Net £209,230 into this company.

The wording is worth noting: money arriving is labelled *payback*, which would mean Fishbone
Properties is repaying a debt it already owed this company rather than lending new money. If
that is right, the direction of the intercompany balance matters a great deal and this company
has been consuming an asset, not incurring a liability. Nothing in this knowledge base
establishes which it is. The reference text on a bank line is not evidence of the legal
character of the payment.

### Why this changes the framing

Earlier work in this knowledge base concentrated on the lending facility - whether FlexiPay was
amortising, whether the funding gap was structural. It was, and it is. But that facility
accounted for minus £22,104 over six months against a £497,071 requirement. **The lender
question is second order.** The first-order question is how long the group and the directors
can keep putting in £53,000 a month, and whether they understand that is what they are doing.

Two consequences follow that this knowledge base cannot resolve alone:

- **Both sides of these balances need to agree.** Fishbone Properties Ltd has its own knowledge
  base, and the group Loans database tracks related-party loans. If the intercompany balance is
  recorded differently in two places, one of them is wrong.
- **This is a group-level question, not a company-level one.** Per `CLAUDE.md` §0 this knowledge
  base exists to pass findings upward to Fishbone Group. This is the clearest candidate.

## Open questions

- **Do Fishbone Properties Ltd and the directors know they are funding roughly £53,000 a
  month?** Nothing here shows the arrangement was ever discussed or documented.
- Are any of these loans documented - terms, interest, repayment date, security? None is
  referenced in this knowledge base.
- Is "Loan Payback" from Fishbone Properties the repayment of an existing debt to this company,
  or new lending mislabelled? The answer inverts the balance sheet treatment.
- Does Fishbone Properties Ltd have the capacity to continue, and what does its own knowledge
  base say about the same transactions?
- Sebastian Pabis appears twice across group records: £50,506 owed by this company per
  QuickBooks A/P, and a £35,000 related-party loan to Fishbone Properties Ltd per the group
  Loans database on which no payment has ever been made. Either two exposures of about £85,000
  combined, or one debt recorded against the wrong entity.
