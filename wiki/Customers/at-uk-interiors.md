# AT UK Interiors Ltd

**Draft: this article exists to record an unresolved classification, not to describe a known
relationship.** AT UK Interiors appears in the bank statements both as what looks like a
customer and as what looks like a related party, and £25,447 of six-month receipts depends on
which it is.

## Key facts

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Received 22 Feb - 21 Mar 2026 | £10,447.61, reference FC0204 | [^1] |
| Received 22 Jul - 21 Aug 2026 | £15,000.00 | [^2] |
| Total | £25,447.61 | |
| Classified here as | Customer receipts, where a job reference is present | - |
| Confidence | **Low. Unconfirmed.** | - |

## Details

The March payment carries **FC0204**, which follows the company's own job-reference convention
(FC + number), as used on Macdonald Joinery payments and on supplier invoices for the Goathland
project. A job reference on an incoming payment normally means work invoiced.

Against that, transactions involving this counterparty were classified as group lending in an
earlier pass over the same data, and the name appears in a context that suggested a related
entity rather than a customer. That earlier classification may have been wrong, or this one may
be.

The two payments are also 22 weeks apart with nothing between them, which fits neither a
regular customer nor a regular intercompany funding pattern well.

**The current treatment is a judgement, not a finding.** It is recorded here so that the next
person to look does not have to re-derive the ambiguity, and so that any figure resting on it
carries the caveat.

### Why it matters

- £25,447 is 12% of six-month third-party customer receipts. If it is not customer income, the
  trading picture is worse than [Trading receipts](../Finance/trading-receipts.md) shows, and
  the August "recovery" largely disappears.
- If it *is* a related party, it belongs in
  [Related-party funding](../Finance/related-party-funding.md) and adds a sixth entity to that
  list.
